# Human clock: sources

The app adds up human time live. Every interaction the agents perform adds the typical elapsed calendar time (waiting included) that a human team takes for that same step. Buyer steps are sequential. Each vendor track, and the analyst research, run in parallel. Values are in `public/humanclock.js`. Rows marked *estimate* are splits or proxies with no direct measurement (about 7% of the total).

On a full deal (47 interactions) the model gives about **85 days to signature**, in line with the 84-day median B2B SaaS sales cycle (the mean is 134 days), and about **106 days end to end** including PO, payment and provisioning.

## Sanity check (from the research)

Adding the events up in strict sequence, with the two vendor tracks running in parallel (counted once): analyst prep (task 24 + 3 searches 24 + 4 reads 16 + brief 80) = 144h. One vendor track (rfp 336 + channel 120 + proposal 264 + questionnaire 120 + grade 170) = 1,010h. Negotiation (5 counters and 5 offers at 48h each = 480h, plus 3 to 10 blocks at 24h each = 72 to 240h) = 552 to 720h. Then redline 168 + award 168 + escalate 24 + approve 115 + signature 24 = 499h. That gives about 2,205 to 2,373h to signature, or 92 to 99 calendar days. After signature, fraud 24 + po 24 + invoice 24 + payment 221 + provision 240 = 533h (22 days), so the full close-to-live clock reads about 2,738 to 2,906h, or 114 to 121 days. The fair comparison with the 134-day average and 84-day median B2B SaaS sales cycle is the signature point, since those cycles end at closed-won. At 92 to 99 days the model sits between the median and the mean. The model is also fully serial, while real teams usually run the analyst brief alongside vendor proposals and run legal redlines alongside commercial negotiation. Overlapping just those two (312h) gives 79 to 86 days, which lands on the 84-day median. As a second check, the slice from negotiation through signature (1,051 to 1,219h, 44 to 51 days) is close to the 35 to 43 day buyer-side close and contract figures in the findings (Ironclad 35 days to execute, Vendr about 41 days, Vertice about 40 days for a new purchase). Those benchmarks exclude a formal RFP, which here adds about 25 days of RFP drafting plus proposal writing. The weakest links are search, read, brief, eliminate and invoice. They are unsourced splits or proxies and should be labeled that way if a judge asks, but together they make up only about 7% of the total.
